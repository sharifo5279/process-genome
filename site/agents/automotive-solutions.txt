# Automotive solutions on Trading Grid

Source: Business Network Process Genome, Solution overview for Automotive (https://process-genome.onrender.com/#automotive/solution).
Status: Concept preview for internal review. Diagrams are illustrative. Some capabilities shown are in development or planned and are not generally available. Results depend on implementation and partner participation. Names, scope and availability may change.

OEM and supplier trading relationships, connected and managed. Integrate and onboard the OEMs, supplier tiers and logistics providers you trade with on Trading Grid, and monitor every release, ship notice and settlement.

## Solutions by stage

- Plan and release: Release management; Sub-tier supplier collaboration
- Ship and deliver: ASN and label compliance; Just-in-sequence monitoring; Inbound freight visibility
- Receive and settle: Cumulative quantity reconciliation; Self-billing and debit memo management
- Assets and aftermarket: Returnable container management; Aftermarket parts data and order management

### Plan and release
Schedules and sub-tier releases aligned before parts ship.

#### Release management
- Purpose: Act on every release change
- Summary: Load planning and shipping schedules as they arrive, compare each release with the last, and flag changes inside firm periods.
- Capabilities:
  - Release changes compared release over release, by part and plant
  - Changes inside firm periods or above agreed limits flagged
  - Missing or late schedules detected before the shipping window
- Parties: OEMs, Suppliers
- Outcome: Release changes visible before parts ship.

#### Sub-tier supplier collaboration
- Purpose: Extend release discipline to sub-tiers
- Summary: Issue your own releases to sub-tier suppliers as OEM demand changes, and hold them to the ship notice and settlement rules the OEM applies to you.
- Capabilities:
  - Releases issued to sub-tier suppliers as OEM demand changes
  - Web access for sub-tier suppliers without EDI
  - Sub-tier scorecards on timeliness and accuracy
- Parties: Tier 1 suppliers, Sub-tier suppliers
- Outcome: Sub-tier suppliers on the same rules.

### Ship and deliver
Ship notices, sequences and freight tracked to the plant.

#### ASN and label compliance
- Purpose: Send an accepted ship notice for every shipment
- Summary: Check each ship notice against the customer's guide before it leaves, match it to labels and the carrier pickup, and act on rejections quickly.
- Capabilities:
  - Ship notices validated against each customer's guide
  - Notices matched to labels and the carrier pickup
  - Rejections explained with the field to correct
- Parties: Suppliers, Carriers, OEM plants
- Outcome: Fewer ship notice debits.

#### Just-in-sequence monitoring
- Purpose: Deliver parts in build order
- Summary: Receive sequence messages as vehicles pass the broadcast point, track each build against its delivery window, and alert on gaps in the sequence.
- Capabilities:
  - Sequence messages monitored for gaps and delays
  - Sequence ship notices matched to the vehicles they supply
  - Plant-critical alerts routed to on-call teams
- Parties: Sequencing suppliers, Assembly plants
- Outcome: Earlier warning of sequence gaps.

#### Inbound freight visibility
- Purpose: Collect parts in the planned window
- Summary: Follow routing instructions, tenders and status events for each inbound shipment, and flag the risks that lead to premium freight.
- Capabilities:
  - Tenders, status events and freight bills linked to the shipment
  - Missed pickup windows flagged as they occur
  - Freight bills compared with tendered rates
- Parties: Suppliers, Logistics providers, Carriers
- Outcome: Earlier warning of premium freight.

### Receive and settle
Receipts, cums and payments reconciled.

#### Cumulative quantity reconciliation
- Purpose: Keep cum totals in agreement
- Summary: Compare shipped and received cumulative quantities by part, and resolve differences before they reach payment or balance-out.
- Capabilities:
  - Supplier and OEM cums compared from ship notices, receipts and the cum on each release
  - Differences traced to the ship notices and receipts behind them
  - Cum resets and balance-out claims supported with history
- Parties: Suppliers, OEMs
- Outcome: Fewer quantity disputes at reconciliation.

#### Self-billing and debit memo management
- Purpose: Get paid for every receipt
- Summary: Match receipts, self-billed invoices and remittances to shipments, and work debit memos inside their dispute windows.
- Capabilities:
  - Receipts and settlements matched to ship notices
  - Short payments and debit memos linked to the shipment
  - Dispute windows tracked with evidence attached
- Parties: OEMs, Suppliers
- Outcome: Fewer debits left open past their dispute window.

### Assets and aftermarket
Container balances reconciled and aftermarket product data kept current.

#### Returnable container management
- Purpose: Keep the container account in balance
- Summary: Track returnable container shipments, receipts and balances by type and site, and reconcile them before the periodic count.
- Capabilities:
  - Container movements tracked from ship notices
  - Balances by container type and site
  - Differences flagged before the periodic count
- Parties: OEMs, Suppliers
- Outcome: Container balances you can reconcile.

#### Aftermarket parts data and order management
- Purpose: Ship the right part with the right data
- Summary: Keep product and fitment data current with distributors, retailers and dealers, confirm orders, and reduce deductions.
- Capabilities:
  - Product and fitment data checked before it is published
  - Orders, acknowledgments and ship notices monitored by customer
  - Deductions linked to the order and shipment
- Parties: Parts suppliers, Distributors, Dealers
- Outcome: Cleaner orders from current product data.

## What OpenText provides
Connectivity, supplier onboarding, visibility, analytics and AI agents across automotive processes.

### Connect and onboard
- Automotive connectivity: OFTP2, AS2 and VAN connections to OEMs and suppliers, with certificates managed.
- Pre-connected community: Reach OEMs, suppliers and carriers already on the network.
- Guided supplier onboarding: Pre-built templates and APIs, with documents tested against each customer's guide.
- Web access for smaller suppliers: Suppliers without EDI receive schedules, send ship notices and invoices, and print labels online.

### See and recover
- Transaction search and recovery: Find any document, follow its processing flow, and redeliver or reprocess it.
- Plant-critical monitoring: Alerts when an expected schedule or ship notice is late for a plant's cutoff.
- Part traceability: Search lot and serial numbers from ship notices to support recall and quality investigations.
- E-invoicing compliance: Invoices issued in the formats tax authorities require in supported countries, as a managed service.

### Measure and improve
- Agents that monitor your rules: Describe a rule in plain language; agents check transactions and explain each finding.
- Supplier delivery scorecards: Score suppliers on release adherence, ship notice accuracy and on-time delivery.
- Ready-made automotive analytics: Dashboards for release volatility, cum differences and ship notice health.
- Governed data delivery: Network data explored in place or delivered to your own analytics on a schedule.

### Act with AI agents
- Errors explained and routed: Agents read a document error, explain it in business terms and route it to the team that fixes it.
- Root cause across failures: Related failures grouped to one cause, with the follow-up to each partner drafted.
- Agents in your tools: Ask about releases, shipments and debits from the assistants your teams already use.
- People approve every change: Agents investigate and recommend; a person approves any resend, update or ticket.
