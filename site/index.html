Reference

Team profile

  • Operations function. Monitoring, support, and on-call beside project work.
  • Partner-controlled timelines. Hubs and regulators set versions, rules, and dates.
  • Upstream root causes. Master data and order entry errors cause many rejections.
  • Missing documents. A document that never arrives raises no error.
  • Provider-delivered work. The team owns outcomes and manages the provider.
  • Team size. From one shared role to a 24x7 roster.

Partner types

    Key pressures

      Core documents

      Stakeholders

      • Customer service and sales ops. Order status, missing orders, escalations.
      • Finance and treasury. Rejected invoices, remittances, payment files, disputes.
      • Supply chain and operations. Ship notices, schedules, labels, carrier data.
      • Master data. Item, location, and partner code fixes.
      • Compliance. Mandate readiness, traceability data, audit evidence.
      • ERP, IT, and security. Upgrades, releases, certificates, access.
      • Leadership. Health reporting, onboarding progress, cost.

      Measured on

      • Tickets within SLA. Response and resolution times.
      • Onboarding on schedule. Against plan and go-live dates.
      • First-pass acceptance. Documents accepted without rejection.
      • Partner scorecards. Timeliness, accuracy, compliance.
      • Cost of EDI. Provider fees and headcount.
